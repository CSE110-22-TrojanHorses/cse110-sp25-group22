Class: NavBar

NavBar()

Masterfully handcrafted Custom Web Component representing the navigation bar. Gives you two buttons: one to go home and one to start “creating” things.

Constructor

new NavBar()

Constructs the navigation bar, creates two buttons (Home and Create), links a stylesheet (which better have your styles), and injects it all into the Shadow DOM for a clean styling sandbox.
